# Space Medicine: Protecting Human Health Beyond Earth
Huamn space exploration is not only an engineering challenge. It is also a biological and medical one. As mission extend beyond Earth's obrit toward the Moon and eventually towards Mars, understanding how the human body to space becomes more and more important. 
## The Human Body in Space
The human body has evolved under Earth's gravity, atmopshere, and radiation environment. In space, astronauts experience conditions that can affect multiple physiological systems. Microgravity can contribute to muscle loss, decreased bone density, and cardiovascular changes. Outside Earth's protective environment and gravity, radiation exposure also becomes an important concern for long duration space missions.
These challenges maje space medicine an interdisiplinary field. Physicians and researchers must pull on physiology, biology, neuroscience, engineering, radiation sciences and more to understand how humans can safey live and work beyond Earth.
